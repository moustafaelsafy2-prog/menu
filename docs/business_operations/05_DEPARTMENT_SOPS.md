# BUSINESS OS: DEPARTMENT STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES (SOPs)

## 1. CEO / Executive SOP
*   **Daily:** Review MRR, New Signups, Churn, and System Uptime.
*   **Weekly:** Executive team meeting (L10 format). Review hiring pipeline and cash runway.
*   **Monthly:** Send investor update. Review financial burn rate vs. budget.
*   **Quarterly:** Board meeting. Strategic pivot assessment.

## 2. Sales SOP
*   **Daily:** Clear tasks in CRM. Follow up on all MQLs within 5 minutes of form submission.
*   **Discovery Calls:** Strictly follow the BANT framework (Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline).
*   **Demos:** Never show a blank dashboard. Always prepopulate the demo account with the prospect's actual logo and menu items scraped from their website.
*   **Handoff:** Upon Closed Won, fill out the internal Handoff Document and introduce the client to their CSM via email.

## 3. Marketing SOP
*   **Campaign Launch:** All campaigns must have tracking links (UTMs) integrated with Mixpanel/HubSpot.
*   **Content:** Publish 2 high-quality SEO articles per week targeting specific long-tail keywords (e.g., "Foodics integration digital menu").
*   **Social Proof:** Automatically request a G2/Capterra review via email 30 days after a user upgrades to Pro.

## 4. Customer Success (CS) SOP
*   **Onboarding:** Host the 30-minute kickoff call. Set up the first branch.
*   **Check-ins:** Day 14, Day 30, and Day 90 touchpoints.
*   **QBRs (Quarterly Business Reviews):** For Enterprise clients, present a slide deck showing their exact ROI (views, time saved).

## 5. Support SOP
*   **Triage:** Acknowledge all tickets within 15 minutes (automated).
*   **Escalation:** If a bug is reproduced, create a Linear ticket, tag the PM, and inform the user: "Our engineering team is reviewing this."
*   **Resolution:** Never close a ticket without confirming the user is satisfied. Update the Knowledge Base if the same question is asked > 3 times.

## 6. Finance SOP
*   **Accounts Receivable:** Run Dunning campaigns for failed Stripe payments on days 3, 5, and 7.
*   **Accounts Payable:** Process vendor invoices on the 15th and 30th.
*   **Reconciliation:** Close the books by the 5th of the following month. Calculate precise CAC and LTV.

## 7. Product Management SOP
*   **Prioritization:** Use the RICE framework (Reach, Impact, Confidence, Effort) to score the backlog.
*   **PRDs:** No engineering work begins without a 1-pager PRD (Product Requirements Document) defining the *Why* and the *Metrics for Success*.
*   **Release:** Draft the changelog and coordinate with Marketing for feature announcements.

## 8. Engineering & Design SOP
*(Note: Technical execution is covered in the Engineering Bible and Design System. This covers operational process).*
*   **Design Handoff:** Figma files must have a "Ready for Dev" page. All tokens must be mapped.
*   **Deployments:** Tuesdays and Thursdays only. No Friday deployments.
*   **Post-Mortems:** Any P0 incident requires a blameless post-mortem document within 48 hours detailing the root cause and the "5 Whys".

## 9. Operations & HR SOP
*   **Onboarding (Internal):** New hires must have laptop, email, Slack, and Github access provisioned 48 hours before Day 1.
*   **Offboarding:** Revoke all SSO access within 5 minutes of termination via the central Identity Provider (Okta/Google Workspace).
*   **Compliance:** Annual data security and privacy training for all employees.
