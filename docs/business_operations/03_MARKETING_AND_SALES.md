# BUSINESS OS: MARKETING, SALES & CRM

## 1. The Marketing Funnel
Our marketing strategy transitions restaurants from physical reliance to digital reliance.
*   **Top of Funnel (TOFU) - Awareness:**
    *   *Channels:* Instagram/TikTok Ads (Visuals of beautiful menus), SEO ("Best digital menu for restaurants"), Local PR.
    *   *Lead Magnet:* Free eBook: "How to increase table turnover by 15%".
*   **Middle of Funnel (MOFU) - Consideration:**
    *   *Channels:* Retargeting ads, Email drip campaigns, Webinar demos.
    *   *Action:* Visitor inputs email to view a live interactive demo of a luxury menu.
*   **Bottom of Funnel (BOFU) - Conversion:**
    *   *Channels:* Direct Sales calls, aggressive free-trial offers.
    *   *Action:* Credit card captured, 14-day trial begins.

## 2. The Sales Funnel & Strategy
We employ a bifurcated sales model based on Customer Lifetime Value (LTV).
*   **Product-Led Growth (PLG):** Small cafes and single-location restaurants are strictly self-serve. No sales team touches them. Marketing drives them to the self-serve signup.
*   **Sales-Led Growth (SLG):** Restaurant chains (3+ locations) and hotels are routed to the Account Executive (AE) team.

## 3. CRM Pipeline (HubSpot / Salesforce)
The SLG pipeline is strictly managed via CRM stages:
1.  **Lead (MQL):** Marketing Qualified Lead. Submitted form for a demo.
2.  **Discovery Call (SQL):** Sales Qualified Lead. BDR (Business Dev Rep) confirms budget and timeline.
3.  **Demo Completed:** AE performs a custom demo using the prospect's actual branding.
4.  **Proposal Sent:** Custom Enterprise pricing sent.
5.  **Negotiation:** Legal and security review (for hotel groups).
6.  **Closed Won:** Handed off to Customer Success Manager (CSM).
7.  **Closed Lost:** Added to long-term nurture drip campaign.

## 4. Lead Scoring
We score leads automatically to prevent wasting AE time.
*   **Demographic Score:** +20 for "CEO/Owner" title. +50 for >5 locations. -100 for "Student" or free email domains (e.g., @gmail.com).
*   **Behavioral Score:** +10 for visiting Pricing page twice. +20 for downloading the eBook. +50 for requesting a demo.
*   **Action:** Any lead reaching a score of 100 is immediately routed to the AE's phone/Slack for instant outreach.

## 5. Customer Segmentation
Once onboarded, customers are segmented to deliver relevant marketing and support.
*   **Segment A (The Whales):** Top 5% of MRR. High touch, QBRs, dedicated Slack channels.
*   **Segment B (The Core):** Standard Pro users. Tech-touch via automated emails, webinars, and in-app tooltips.
*   **Segment C (The Long Tail):** Basic users. Zero touch. 100% self-serve support via Knowledge Base.
*   **Segment D (At-Risk):** Usage drop > 30% week-over-week. Triggers automated intervention sequence.
