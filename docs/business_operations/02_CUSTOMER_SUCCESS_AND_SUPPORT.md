# BUSINESS OS: CUSTOMER SUCCESS & SUPPORT

## 1. The Customer Lifecycle
Our philosophy: A restaurant owner is not an IT expert. The software must serve them, not burden them.
1.  **Awareness -> Acquisition -> Activation -> Retention -> Expansion -> Advocacy.**
2.  The critical metric is **Time-to-Value (TTV)**: The time from signup to a diner scanning the first QR code.

## 2. Onboarding & Activation Strategy
*   **Concierge Onboarding (Pro/Enterprise):** A Customer Success Manager (CSM) manually builds the restaurant's first menu from a PDF. This guarantees 100% activation but costs internal labor.
*   **Self-Serve Onboarding (Basic):** In-app checklist. Progress bar (e.g., "Menu 80% Complete").
*   **The "Aha!" Moment:** Triggered when the owner views their own beautifully designed digital menu for the first time.

## 3. Customer Support Infrastructure
*   **Ticketing System:** Intercom or Zendesk. All channels (Email, Social, Chat) route to one unified inbox.
*   **Live Chat:** Active in the B2B Dashboard. AI bot handles tier-1 triage (reset password, how to add item). Hands off to human for complex POS integration issues.
*   **Knowledge Base:** Comprehensive, video-first documentation. Owners prefer watching a 30-second loom over reading a 10-page manual.
*   **Community:** A private Slack/Discord for Enterprise owners to discuss industry trends, acting as a retention moat.

## 4. Service Level Agreements (SLA)
*   **Enterprise:** 15-minute response time. 99.99% uptime guarantee with financial penalties (credits) if breached.
*   **Pro:** 2-hour response time during business hours.
*   **Basic:** 24-hour email response.

## 5. Retention & Offboarding
*   **Retention Strategy:** Quarterly Business Reviews (QBRs) for Enterprise clients. Automated monthly PDF reports for Pro users showing "Money Saved on Printing" and "Most Popular Items."
*   **Offboarding:** Data export rights. Graceful, frictionless exit to leave the door open for future win-backs.

## 6. Expansion, Upselling, & Cross-selling
*   **Expansion:** Expanding from 1 branch to 5 branches.
*   **Upsell:** Moving a user from Basic ($29) to Pro ($79) by feature-gating advanced analytics. When they click "Analytics", show a blurred chart and an "Upgrade" button.
*   **Cross-sell:** Selling NFC physical table pucks or custom acrylic QR stands directly from the dashboard.

## 7. Lifecycle Automation Strategies
We utilize a multi-channel automated communication engine (e.g., Customer.io).

### 7.1. Email Automation
*   **Day 1:** Welcome + "Book your onboarding call".
*   **Day 3:** "Did you know you can add dietary tags?" (Value realization).
*   **Day 10 (If trial unactivated):** "Need help building your menu? Reply here."
*   **Day 30:** First Monthly Analytics Report.

### 7.2. WhatsApp Automation
*   Primary channel for the Middle East.
*   Automated alerts for billing failures (Dunning). "Your card expired. Update here to keep your menu online: [Link]". High read rate ensures minimal involuntary churn.

### 7.3. SMS & Push Automation
*   **SMS:** Reserved strictly for critical system alerts (e.g., "POS Sync disconnected").
*   **Push Notifications:** Diner-facing only (future phase) for order status updates. Not used for B2B.
