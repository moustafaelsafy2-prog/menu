# BUSINESS OS: HANDBOOKS

## 1. The Company Handbook (The North Star)
*   **Mission:** To make hospitality frictionless.
*   **Core Values:**
    1.  *Speed over Everything:* In our software and our execution.
    2.  *Default to Transparency:* Share information openly internally.
    3.  *Customer Obsession:* The restaurant owner's success is our only metric of success.
*   **The "Why":** We exist because dining out should be about the food and the company, not waving down a waiter for 15 minutes to ask for a menu.

## 2. The Employee Handbook (Internal Operations)
*   **Communication:** Slack is for ephemeral chat; Notion is for permanent decisions. If it's not in Notion, it didn't happen.
*   **Working Hours:** We are an async-first, remote-friendly team. Overlap core hours are 10 AM to 2 PM (Riyadh Time) for meetings.
*   **PTO Policy:** "Take what you need, but don't leave your team stranded." Minimum 20 days required.
*   **Hardware:** $2,500 budget for laptop and home office setup upon hiring.

## 3. The Customer Handbook (External - Knowledge Base)
*   **Format:** A public-facing Help Center.
*   **Tone:** Encouraging, simple, non-technical.
*   **Key Sections:**
    *   *Getting Started:* "How to build your first menu in 10 minutes."
    *   *Hardware:* "Which printers work with SmartMenu?"
    *   *Best Practices:* "How to take photos that sell more food."

## 4. The Partner Handbook
*   **Audience:** POS integrators, hardware vendors.
*   **Content:** Outlines the API documentation, SLA expectations, and co-marketing guidelines.
*   **Rule of Engagement:** "Do no harm to the restaurant." Any partner integration that slows down the SmartMenu platform will be immediately disconnected.

## 5. The Agency Handbook
*   **Audience:** Digital marketing agencies managing restaurants.
*   **Content:** How to use the Agency Dashboard. How to bulk-manage themes. How to export analytics for end-of-month client reporting.
*   **Revenue Share:** Outlines the exact mechanics of the 20% commission payout structure and lead-registration process.
