# PRODUCT ROADMAP: VERSIONS 2.0 TO 5.0

## VERSION 2.0 (The Transactional Pivot)
*   **Goals:** Shift from a SaaS model to a SaaS + Fintech model. Enable diners to order and pay directly from the digital menu.
*   **Business Value:** Exponential (Unlocks GMV take-rate revenue).
*   **Technical Complexity:** Extreme (PCI compliance, real-time Kitchen Display System routing, complex modifier math).
*   **Estimated Development Effort:** 6 Months.
*   **Dependencies:** V1.5 POS Sync (Orders must reach the kitchen).
*   **Release Criteria:** 100% successful order routing to POS/Printers. Sub-second payment processing.
*   **KPIs:** GMV Processed, Average Order Value (AOV), Payment Success Rate.
*   **Risks:** A dropped order in a restaurant is catastrophic. The fail-safes must be flawless.
*   **Success Metrics:** 10% of existing SaaS base activates payments.

### Features
| Feature | Priority | User Impact | Revenue Impact | Difficulty | Future Dependencies |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Table-side Ordering** | P0 | Critical | Extreme| High | V2.5 Bill Splitting |
| **Apple Pay / Google Pay**| P0 | High | High | Med | V4.0 Loyalty Points Pay |
| **Order Routing to POS** | P0 | High (Staff) | Low | Extreme | V5.0 Ghost Kitchen OS |
