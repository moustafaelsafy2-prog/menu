# PRODUCT ROADMAP: DOMAIN ROADMAPS (5-YEAR HORIZON)

## 1. Technical Roadmap
*   **Year 1:** Monolithic core with decoupled Edge frontend. PostgreSQL primary.
*   **Year 2:** Transition transactional engine to event-driven microservices (Kafka) to handle Payment throughput.
*   **Year 3:** Implement ClickHouse for OLAP analytics. Shard PostgreSQL by region to comply with data residency laws.
*   **Year 4:** Introduce Machine Learning pipelines for predictive upselling.
*   **Year 5:** Deprecate web-only focus. Release native iOS/Android SDKs for Enterprise chains to embed SmartMenu into their own apps.

## 2. Business Roadmap
*   **Year 1:** Achieve PMF (Product-Market Fit). Hit $1M ARR. Prove low churn.
*   **Year 2:** Launch native payments. Convert 30% of SaaS base to Fintech base. Reach profitability.
*   **Year 3:** Expand across the entire GCC (UAE, Qatar, Kuwait). Hit $10M ARR.
*   **Year 4:** Launch Coalition Loyalty network. Monetize diner data (anonymized trends to FMCG brands).
*   **Year 5:** Prepare for acquisition by global POS player or IPO on Nomu (Saudi parallel market).

## 3. Marketing Roadmap
*   **Year 1:** Guerrilla marketing. Free setups. B2B content marketing (SEO).
*   **Year 2:** Shift to Partner/Channel sales. Sponsor major hospitality expos (e.g., GulfHost).
*   **Year 3:** B2C brand building. "Look for the SmartMenu badge for a premium dining experience."
*   **Year 4:** Performance marketing to diners to download the universal discovery app.
*   **Year 5:** Global brand campaigns.

## 4. Infrastructure Roadmap
*   **Year 1:** PaaS heavily utilized (Vercel, managed databases) to optimize for developer velocity.
*   **Year 2:** Migrate core compute to self-hosted Kubernetes (EKS) to control costs as volume scales.
*   **Year 3:** Multi-region active-active deployment (AWS ME-South + EU-Central).
*   **Year 4:** Custom Edge nodes deployed inside major partner hotel networks (On-premise hybrid caching).
*   **Year 5:** Serverless transition for bursty workloads (Dinner rush scaling).

## 5. Hiring Roadmap
*   **Year 1:** 2 Founders, 3 Senior Engineers, 1 Product Designer, 1 Head of Sales. (Scrappy, high equity).
*   **Year 2:** Hire VP of Engineering, build out Customer Success team (5 FTEs), hire localized sales reps in UAE.
*   **Year 3:** Build specialized Data Science team. Hire Chief Revenue Officer (CRO). Total headcount ~50.
*   **Year 4:** Build out legal and compliance teams for Fintech regulatory adherence. Total headcount ~120.
*   **Year 5:** Executive team reshuffle for public markets/acquisition. Total headcount ~250.

## 6. Investment Roadmap
*   **Pre-Seed ($500k):** Funds Year 1. Used strictly for product development and initial GTM testing.
*   **Seed ($3M):** Triggers at $1M ARR (Year 2). Used to fund the expensive Payment/Fintech engineering pivot and UAE expansion.
*   **Series A ($12M):** Triggers at $5M ARR (Year 3). Used to dominate the GCC market and launch the B2C Coalition Loyalty network.
*   **Series B ($30M+):** Triggers at $15M ARR. Used for global expansion (Europe/Asia) or strategic acquisitions of smaller POS companies.
